## Why this studio exists

The strongest agents already live on your laptop — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, and the rest. The missing piece was never another model. It's **software rebuilt to assume AI from line one** — tools you fully own, that keep running long after the agent is done.

Most "AI features" are a chatbot stapled to software designed before agents existed. We do the opposite: pick a category, rethink it from first principles, ship it open. **AI does the hard work once — then you own and run the result, forever, without the AI tax.**

Local-first, open by default. Apache-2.0 / MIT, no telemetry, no lock-in.

## Principles

- **AI-native, not bolted-on.** We don't wrap a chatbot around old software. We rebuild it assuming AI from line one.
- **Rebuild, don't retrofit.** Every category of software is worth redoing now. We pick one, rethink it, ship it open.
- **Open by default.** Apache-2.0 / MIT across the stack. No locked surface, no telemetry.
- **Local-first.** Your code, your machine, your keys. Cloud second, never first.
- **AI authors it; you own and run it.** Let AI do the work once, then ship something that runs without it — no AI tax.
